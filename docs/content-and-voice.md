banners).

**"Chill streams, gaming with friends."** Game lists end with "and more" rather than enumerating.

only said by her. Lines are shuffled per form (no repeats until the list is exhausted, never the same line
twice in a row). Required lines: **"GGs."** (cyan Tron) and **"I am not a delayed gamer!"** (red Tron).
