Botrix widgets

How this works

Paste each link from Botrix's widget page. They're kept in this browser only, never on the website. The scenes then show the widgets inside their frames, and each card's Copy includes them, so in OBS the scene is the only source you need (no Botrix sources or Custom CSS). Botrix puts the settings in the link, so copy it again after changing them.

Copy bookmark link gives this page's address with both links packed onto the end (after the #). Bookmark it, or open it in another browser or on another computer, and the links fill themselves in. The packed part never leaves your browser, but it's only scrambled, not locked: treat the bookmark as privately as the Botrix links themselves.
