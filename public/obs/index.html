How this works

Paste each link from Botrix's widget page. They're kept in this browser only, never on the website. The scenes then show the widgets inside their frames, and each card's Copy includes them, so in OBS the scene is the only source you need (no Botrix sources or Custom CSS). Botrix puts the settings in the link, so copy it again after changing them.

Copy bookmark link gives this page's address with both links packed onto the end (after the #). Bookmark it, or open it in another browser or on another computer, and the links fill themselves in. The packed part (after the #, never a ?) never leaves your browser, but it's only scrambled, not locked: treat the bookmark as privately as the Botrix links themselves.

Or keep the links in Netlify (env vars BOTRIX_CHAT_URL, BOTRIX_GOAL_URL, plus a password of your choosing in OBS_KEY) and type that key here instead. Copy then gives each scene with ?key=…, and the scene fetches the current links from Netlify when it loads, so changing a link in Netlify updates every OBS source. A link pasted above still wins for its frame. The key can also go in this page's address: /obs/#key=… (or ?key=…).
