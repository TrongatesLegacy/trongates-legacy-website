How it works

  1. 1

    Letters appear

    A scrambled word shows on stream and reshuffles every few seconds.

  2. 2

    Chat types words

    Anyone on Twitch or Kick types words they can make from the letters. First to find one gets the points.

  3. 3

    Beat the goal

    Find enough words before time runs out to clear the level. Longer words, trickier letters, bigger scores.

    FAQ

    Is it really free?

    Yes. No account, no login, no catch. It's a web page you add to OBS as a browser source. The small “Made by” line can be turned off in Advanced settings.

    Does it need my Twitch or Kick password?

    No. It only reads your public chat, the way a viewer does, and never posts anything.

    Can I use it with just Kick, or just Twitch?

    Yes: fill in one channel or both. Streaming to both at once? Everyone plays on the same board, and the summary shows which chat found more.

    Will its commands clash with my other bots?

    They all start with !cg (!cg start, !cg top…), so they rarely do, and you can rename them in Advanced settings. Bots like Botrix and Nightbot are ignored as players out of the box.

    How do the leaderboards work?

    Type !cg top (or just !cg) to show this stream's and all-time top players for a few seconds. This stream starts fresh each time you go live: Chatagram asks Twitch or Kick whether you're live, so a crash and a quick restart still count as one stream. All time is kept for good. You can hide it in Advanced settings, or wipe both with !cg clearscores (only you can).

    What happens if OBS refreshes mid-game?

    It carries on where it was: the game and the leaderboards are saved in OBS's browser as it goes.

    Does it work in Streamlabs, Meld or XSplit?

    Anything with a browser source works.

    Where do the words come from?

    The SCOWL English word lists (by how common each word is), with rude words taken out using SCOWL's own tags, the LDNOOBW list and our own. You can add words to leave out in Advanced settings.
